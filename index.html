The Bot

What PGS actually does

PGS is an Expert Advisor (EA) that runs on MetaTrader inside your own account at your own broker. Your funds stay with your broker — PGS only opens and closes trades according to fixed rules.

Key features

  • Fixed rules, zero discretion — every entry, exit and lot size is defined in advance.
  • Trades EURUSD and XAUUSD on H1 only — a deliberately small set of instruments.
  • Fixed 1% risk per trade with a strict stop-loss on every position.
  • Runs 24/5 on your VPS or PC — you can watch every trade live.
  • Your capital stays at your broker; the bot has no withdrawal access.

What PGS does NOT do — honestly

  • No guaranteed profit and no fixed monthly return — check the worst month in the results.
  • No martingale, no lot multiplication, no grid without stop-loss.
  • Not profitable every single month — losses are part of the system, and the table shows them.
  • No access to your funds: deposits, withdrawals and the account remain under your control only.

FAQ

Everything to know before subscribing

Does PGS guarantee profit?

No — and nobody honestly can. PGS follows fixed rules with controlled risk, but losses are part of trading. That's exactly why the full track record, worst months included, is public.

Do you ever touch my money?

Never. The bot runs inside your MetaTrader account at your broker. We don't take deposits, we don't hold funds, and we have no withdrawal access.

Why rent instead of buying a lifetime license?

A rental keeps us accountable: your fee includes updates, monitoring and support. If the system stops performing, you simply don't renew.

Will it work with my broker?

PGS works with any MT5 broker that allows hedging and Expert Advisors. Send us your broker's name on Discord and we confirm before you pay.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

MetaTrader must run 24/5. Most subscribers use a cheap VPS (about $5–10/month) — we can recommend one and set it up with you.

Does it use martingale?

No. No martingale, no grid without stop-loss. Every position opens with a fixed 1% risk and a defined stop-loss.

What is the minimum balance?

We recommend at least $500. Smaller balances can't absorb normal drawdowns safely.

How does installation work?

After payment you get the PGS file plus a step-by-step guide — or book the free setup session and we install it with you remotely.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your renewal date — no questions asked.
